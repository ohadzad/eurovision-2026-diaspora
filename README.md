# Eurovision 2026 — Israel Televote vs. Jewish/Israeli Diaspora (v2)

Improved analysis cross-referencing the public vote for Israel in the Eurovision 2026 Grand Final (Vienna, 16.5.2026) with both Jewish population and Israeli expat estimates per country.

**Live:** https://ohadzad.github.io/eurovision-2026-israel-vote/

## Structure

1. **Results** — what happened on the night (Israel 2nd with 343 pts; 220 televote)
2. **Empirical / statistical analysis** — Pearson & Spearman correlations, quartile means
3. **Interpretation** — what the data does and doesn't support
4. **Research limitations** — twelve methodological caveats

## Key findings (v2)

- Moderate positive correlation between Jewish+Israeli population and points (r ≈ 0.32, ρ ≈ 0.34)
- Israeli expat count alone shows ρ = 0.47 (stronger rank correlation)
- Country general population correlates +0.34 — major confounder
- Top quartile of Jewish/Israeli population averaged 8.22 pts vs. 6.00 for bottom quartile
- Five countries (Ireland, Spain, Slovenia, Netherlands, Iceland) boycotted

## Changes from v1

v1 reported a Pearson r of ≈0.01 and concluded "no correlation." That was wrong on two counts: (a) ROW was included as an outlier dragging the value down, and (b) Israeli expats — explicitly requested — were ignored. v2 separates ROW, adds Israeli numbers, uses Spearman alongside Pearson, and adds an explicit limitations section.
